# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leads and potential collaborators reviewing Carlos Chile Silva's work. They need to understand his focus, assess representative projects, and reach his LinkedIn or GitHub without having to decode the visual system.

## Product Purpose

Present Carlos as a developer who builds AI systems, agents, data products and full-stack experiences. The portfolio should make the work credible within the first screen and make deeper project exploration easy.

## Brand Personality

Curious, precise and cosmic. The site should feel like a personal observatory: intelligent and memorable without becoming theatrical or hard to read.

## Anti-references

Generic AI gradients, dense dashboards, boxed template cards, decorative motion that obstructs reading, and fantasy motifs that compete with the projects.

## Design Principles

1. Let the work lead: titles, outcomes and links stay clear before visual effects.
2. Make the cosmos functional: motion guides attention, navigation and exploration.
3. Keep one visual voice: dark forest space, ivory ink and one warm celestial accent.
4. Reward depth: filters, search and project details support technical reviewers without overwhelming recruiters.
5. Respect control: visible focus, reduced motion and an optional light theme make the site comfortable to use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, readable body copy and theme preference persistence.
